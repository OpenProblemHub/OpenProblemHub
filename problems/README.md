# Problems

This directory contains **curated, real-world engineering problems**.

These are **not product ideas**, **not startup pitches**, and **not feature requests**.  
They are problems that are:

- Real
- Observable
- Worth serious engineering effort
- Reusable beyond a single team or product

If a problem cannot survive careful scrutiny, it does not belong here.

---

## What Belongs Here

A problem in this repository must satisfy **all** of the following:

1. **It exists in the real world**
   - Someone is experiencing this problem today
   - It is not hypothetical or speculative

2. **It has a clearly defined context**
   - Specific users
   - Specific environments
   - Real constraints

3. **It causes measurable pain**
   - Time, cost, risk, or cognitive load
   - Not just “inconvenience” or “nice to have”

4. **It is not solved well enough**
   - Existing solutions exist but are inadequate
   - Or the problem emerges due to new constraints (scale, AI, tooling)

5. **It is worth engineering effort**
   - Non-trivial technically
   - Likely to produce reusable patterns or tooling

---

## What Does NOT Belong Here

The following will be rejected without discussion:

 ❌ Product ideas or business pitches  
 ❌ “Build an app that…” style submissions  
 ❌ AI wrappers without a concrete underlying problem  
 ❌ Vague complaints without context or constraints  
 ❌ Problems that reduce to “users don’t like X”  
 ❌ Feature requests for existing tools  
 ❌ Market gap speculation without lived experience  

If your submission starts with a solution, it is likely in the wrong place.

---

## Problem-First Principle

This community operates on a strict **problem-first** principle:

> We align on the problem before discussing solutions.

A well-defined problem can have many valid solutions.  
A poorly defined problem makes all solutions irrelevant.

---

## Required Structure

Every problem **must** follow the official template:
```
templates/problem-template.md
```


Submissions that deviate from the template will not be reviewed.

The structure is intentionally demanding.  
If filling out the template reveals gaps in your understanding, that is a feature—not a bug.

---

## Naming Convention

Problem files must follow this format:

```
<concise-problem-slug>.md
```


Examples:

```
runtime-configuration-state-is-not-reproducible.md
llm-apps-lack-observable-user-feedback-loops.md
```


Guidelines:

- Describe the **problem**, not the solution
- Avoid buzzwords and generic terms

---

## Lifecycle

Problems in this repository may move through the following states:

- **Active** — under discussion or analysis
- **Validated** — confirmed by multiple contributors
- **Explored** — experiments or MVPs exist
- **Archived** — no longer relevant or superseded

Archiving a problem is not failure.  
It is a sign of intellectual honesty.

---

## Review Philosophy

Reviewers focus on:

- Clarity of the problem
- Strength of validation signals
- Respect for real-world constraints
- Engineering relevance

We value precision over enthusiasm.

---

## Final Note

This repository is intentionally **not inclusive of all ideas**.

It is inclusive of:
- Careful thinking
- Lived experience
- Engineering discipline

If that resonates with you, you are in the right place.
