# Community Roles

This document defines the roles and responsibilities within this community.

Roles exist to **protect problem quality**, not to create hierarchy.
Authority is earned through demonstrated judgment, not activity volume.

---

## Role Overview

The community operates with three primary roles:

- **Observer**
- **Contributor**
- **Reviewer**

Each role has clearly defined permissions and expectations.
Advancement is **by invitation only**.

---

## 1. Observer

**Who they are**

- Anyone reading or following the repository
- No prior contribution required

**Permissions**

- Read all public content
- Participate in discussions (where enabled)

**Restrictions**

- Cannot submit new problems
- Cannot approve or merge content
- No decision-making authority

**Purpose**

Observers lower the barrier to entry without lowering the quality bar.
They are encouraged to learn the community’s standards before contributing.

---

## 2. Contributor

**Who they are**

- Individuals who submit structured content aligned with community standards
- Typically engineers with direct experience of the problems described

**Permissions**

- Submit new problems using the official template
- Propose revisions to existing problems
- Participate in analysis and discussion
- Submit experiments or supporting materials when appropriate

**Expectations**

- Respect the problem-first principle
- Accept revision or rejection without debate
- Focus on clarity, constraints, and validation

**Restrictions**

- Cannot self-approve submissions
- Cannot override reviewer decisions
- Cannot invite others into higher roles

**Notes**

Contribution volume does not imply seniority.
One high-quality submission outweighs many marginal ones.

---

## 3. Reviewer

**Who they are**

- Trusted contributors with consistent judgment
- Individuals who demonstrate the ability to reject weak problems as well as accept strong ones

**Permissions**

- Review problem submissions
- Request revisions or reject submissions
- Approve problems into the curated set
- Label and manage problem lifecycle states

**Responsibilities**

- Apply the review checklist strictly and consistently
- Protect long-term signal-to-noise ratio
- Prioritize clarity over enthusiasm
- Document reasons for rejection or revision

**Restrictions**

- Cannot approve their own submissions
- Cannot bypass the review checklist
- Cannot promote themselves or others to Reviewer

**Appointment**

Reviewers are invited by existing Reviewers or Maintainers based on:

- Demonstrated judgment
- Respect for constraints and scope
- Ability to say “no” clearly and constructively

---

## Role Advancement

There is **no automatic promotion**.

Advancement follows this pattern:

```
Observer → Contributor → Reviewer
```


Promotion criteria are qualitative, not quantitative.

Examples of signals that may lead to invitation:

- Consistently high-quality submissions
- Thoughtful review comments
- Clear articulation of constraints and trade-offs
- Constructive handling of rejection or revision

---

## Maintainers (Administrative Role)

**Who they are**

- Stewards of the repository structure and governance
- Responsible for enforcement, not content direction

**Responsibilities**

- Maintain repository structure
- Enforce governance documents
- Resolve procedural disputes
- Manage access permissions

Maintainers do **not** override reviewer judgment on problem quality,
except in cases of clear process violation.

---

## Decision-Making Principles

- Problem quality decisions are made by **Reviewers**
- Governance and process decisions are made by **Maintainers**
- No single individual should be a bottleneck

Disagreements should default to **stricter interpretation**, not compromise.

---

## Removal or Demotion

Roles may be revoked if an individual:

- Repeatedly ignores review standards
- Lowers the quality bar intentionally or negligently
- Acts in bad faith or disrupts community trust

Role removal is a governance action, not a personal judgment.

---

## Final Principle

This community values:

- Judgment over speed
- Precision over volume
- Trust over scale

Roles exist to protect these values.
